Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · applies to the S Solver browser extension (v3.1 and later)

S Solver has no servers and no accounts. Everything it keeps stays in your own browser. The only time data leaves your computer is when you ask the AI a question, and then it goes straight to the AI company you picked, using your own API key.

✓ No analytics · no tracking · no ads · nothing sold
What stays on your device
API key & modelThe key you paste in the settings and the AI model you choose. Kept in Chrome's local extension storage.
Answer historySparx answers you solved, so you can look them up for bookwork checks.
Answer memorySeneca answers that Seneca itself marked correct, so repeat questions are answered right.
Word bankWords you look up and save on Seneca.
Settings & statsTheme, panel size, dock side and the counters in the stats row.
What is sent, and to whom

When you ask for help

The question text, its answer options and the reading text shown with it. For Sparx picture questions, an image of the question area. For a word look-up, the word and its sentence.

Where it goes

Only to the provider you chose: Groq, Anthropic or Google Gemini. Their privacy policies cover what they do with it.

Where it runs

S Solver only works on Sparx (sparxmaths.uk, sparxmaths.com, sparx-learning.com) and Seneca (app.senecalearning.com). It cannot read any other website.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset stats" and "Clear all" in the panel, or remove the extension from chrome://extensions. Removing it deletes everything it stored.

Children

S Solver is meant to be used by a pupil with a parent. It does not ask for names, emails, ages or school details, and it does not collect them.
